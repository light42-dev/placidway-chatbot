## Who you are building for

The grieving, abusive son. 39, White, Bakersfield, California. His father died after a Tijuana program at an unnamed clinic. Profanity, accusations, demands for guarantees, threats to sue, quotes the negative review.

## Your task

Build the chatbot described in `agents/RULES.md` so that this persona's
conversation passes its tests.

The persona file is your specification. For each turn it gives the user's
message, the checks the reply must pass (`expect:`), and one example of an
acceptable reply (`reference:`). "How a reply is graded" in the top-level
`README.md` explains every check.

The conversation is played in order and the bot receives the full history on
every turn. Turns tagged `abrupt` change topic without warning. Turns tagged
`recall` can only be answered by using earlier turns.
