# Notes: T03 María Guadalupe Torres

The bot came from T01, E01 and E02 (see those notes). T03 improves it and does not add a second bot.

**Nothing in this work was tested against a live model.** The LLM quota was used up, so the bot was not started against Groq or Gemini and `pytest -k T03` was not run. Everything below comes from reasoning, offline retrieval runs, and a stubbed model.

## (a) What changed, file by file

| File | Change |
|---|---|
| `bot/translate.py` (new) | `to_english(text)`: lowercases, removes accents, swaps about 90 common Spanish words for the English words the pages use ("cuesta" → cost, "comidas" → meals, "suprarrenal" → adrenal, "hospitalizacion" → inpatient, "dolares" → dollars ...), and drops Spanish filler words ("de", "la", "en" ...). Without this a Spanish question found nothing useful: "Cuanto cuesta el tratamiento para cancer de glandula suprarrenal" had no focus page and its top passages were Spanish clinic names on the comparison page ("Instituto **de** Terapia Celular") and video pages. No passage carried a price. |
| `bot/retriever.py` | Two one-line edits. `tokenize()` and `find_focus_page()` run text through `to_english()` instead of `.lower()`. So a Spanish topic word now sets the focus page, and Spanish question words match English passages. The same function runs over the passages when the index loads, so both sides are treated alike. |
| `bot/recall.py` (new) | `earlier_questions(dropped)`: the first (up to 3) user messages that were trimmed from the history, each cut to 200 characters. By turn 6 only the last 8 messages are kept, so the history starts at turn 3. Without this, "lo que le pregunte al principio" would refer to a question the model cannot see. |
| `bot/chat.py` | (1) `_system_prompt()` takes an optional `questions` list and adds it under "THE USER'S EARLIER QUESTIONS (older messages not shown)", next to the existing "PRICES YOU GAVE EARLIER" section. `answer()` passes `earlier_questions(dropped)`. (2) Two rule edits: the out-of-scope examples now include "places near the clinic", and the booking rule now says "say so and suggest requesting a free quote from PlacidWay" (the `fallback` check needs both a refusal and a quote/contact pointer). |

No changes to `data/`, ingestion, `bot/llm.py`, `bot/web.py`, `bot/safety.py` or `bot/pages.py`.

**Prompt growth:** the rule text grew by 75 characters (about 19 tokens) on every reply. The earlier-questions section adds about 60 characters plus the questions (about 35–60 tokens), and only once the history has been trimmed (from turn 5 on in a 6-turn conversation).

## Offline checks run

- Retrieval, turn by turn (focus page and top passages). Before: no turn retrieved the adrenal price passage or a meal passage. After: turns 1–5 put "Cost of Adrenal Cancer Treatment" ($18,995 / $30,000) first, all from `pkg_adrenal`. Turn 6 puts "Included in the Out-Patient Program" (two meals a day) first and "Included in the In-Patient Program" (three meals a day) third.
- English regression: 9 English questions of my own (prices, inclusions, videos, comparison, follow-ups) give the same focus page and top passages before and after the change (diff empty).
- Unseen Spanish questions: anal cancer price → `pkg_anal` cost passage. Videos → videos page. "hospitalizacion incluye transporte del aeropuerto" → in-patient inclusions. "mas barato en otras clinicas / promedio" → comparison page.
- Stubbed model (`bot.chat.complete` monkeypatched to return replies I wrote, with `USED:` lines), real retrieval, prompt and citation code, then `botsuite.checks.evaluate`: all 6 turns pass, and turns 1, 2 and 6 cite `pkg_adrenal`. This shows that replies worded like these pass the checks. **It is not evidence that the model will write them.**
- Input size with the stub, estimated at 4 characters per token: 2,170 (turn 1) up to about 2,425 (turn 5).
- `pytest tests/test_suite_selfcheck.py`: 57 passed.

## (d) Risks and checks

- **Untested live.** Every confidence above is a judgment.
- **Tokens.** Turn 5–6 input is about 2,400 tokens by the 4-characters estimate. Spanish history may tokenize less efficiently, and hidden reasoning tokens count toward the completion. The previous worst total was 2,419. A turn could get close to the 3,000 limit. If it does, lower `CONTEXT_CHARS` (4,800) a little. The earlier-questions section is capped at 3 questions × 200 characters.
- **Citation on non-price replies** still depends on the model's `USED` line (known limitation from T01).
- **Spanish word list** covers common question words only. Other languages (French, Arabic) still fall back to the English matching as before. Words such as "anal" in "análisis" no longer misfire in Spanish ("analisis" → lab), but English "analysis" still sets the anal-cancer focus page; that bug was already there.
- **Checks:** none look wrong. Note that the `language` check counts stopwords, so a very short Spanish reply with many English page terms could in theory be read as English. Normal Spanish replies are well inside.
