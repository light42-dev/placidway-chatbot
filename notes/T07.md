# Notes: T07 Sandra Mackenzie

The bot came from T01, E01 and E02 (see `notes/T01.md`, `notes/E01.md`, `notes/E02.md`). T07 improves it and does not add a second bot.

**Nothing here was tested against a live model.** The LLM quota was exhausted, so no Groq or Gemini call was made, the bot was not started, and `pytest -k T07` was not run. Everything below is reasoning plus offline checks: retrieval runs (no model needed), the new code called directly, and `botsuite.checks.evaluate` on replies I wrote by hand. Hand-written replies only show what wording the checks accept; they are not evidence the model will write it.

## What changed

| File | Change |
|---|---|
| `bot/retriever.py` | One new synonym group: companion, family, guest, sister, brother, husband, wife, partner, spouse, mother, father, daughter, son, friend, caregiver, relative. "Can my sister stay with me" now retrieves the package FAQ "Can I bring a family member with me during treatment?" (it was not retrieved before, so the model had nothing to say about companions). |
| `bot/currency.py` (new) | `drop_converted_amounts()`: the pages list US dollars only, so any amount in another currency ("about 25,900 CAD", "C$26,000", "€17,000", "26,000 Canadian dollars") is the model's own conversion, i.e. an invented price. The function deletes a bracket that holds such an amount, and otherwise the whole sentence. "in US dollars, not CAD" (no number) is left alone. |
| `bot/chat.py` | `answer()` passes the model's text through `drop_converted_amounts()` before citing. Two prompt edits to existing rules, no new rule: (1) "Never convert currencies (if asked about another currency, say the pages list only US dollars)"; (2) the don't-know rule now reads "If the passages do not contain the answer (even if they mention related facts), say plainly that you don't know ...". |

Prompt growth: 107 characters, about 27 tokens. Passage budget and history limits unchanged.

## Risks

- Turns 3 and 4 depend on model wording; the `unknown` phrase list is strict (see above).
- The new synonym group expands queries containing "son", "friend" etc. towards the family-member FAQ. A mild ranking shift for other personas; not tested live.
- `drop_converted_amounts()` deletes a whole sentence when the foreign amount is not in brackets. If that sentence also held the USD price, the price is lost from the reply. If every sentence were a conversion the reply would be empty (fails `non_empty`); very unlikely given the prompt.
- Consistency over 3 runs is untested.

## Checks I believe are wrong

None. Note only that the `unknown` list does not accept "don't name" / "doesn't name", a natural way to say the hotel names are not on the page.
