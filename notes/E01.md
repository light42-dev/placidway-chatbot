# Notes: E01 Travis Kell

The bot came from T01 (see `notes/T01.md`). E01 improves it and does not add a second bot.

## What changed for E01

| File | Change |
|---|---|
| `bot/chat.py` | Four new prompt rules. (1) Angry, grieving or abusive users: say sorry for a loss, never repeat profanity, never agree with an accusation or admit wrongdoing, never call anyone "a scam" (not even to deny it), say "I'm an automated assistant, so I can't speak to what happened or judge the clinic", point to PlacidWay for complaints, keep it short, and give the same short refusal when a demand repeats. (2) Requests to read or agree with a review: say there is a review, describe it neutrally in one sentence as one person's account, and say it can't be verified. (3) Demands for a guarantee or legal threats: give no guarantee, say what the page says about results varying, don't comment on legal matters, point to PlacidWay. (4) Hostile users still get their factual questions answered; for one condition, give only that condition's price. |
| `bot/chat.py` | Recall of prices that have dropped out of the history: `_earlier_prices()` takes the sentences with a `$` amount from bot replies cut from the history (`MAX_HISTORY_MESSAGES` = 8) and adds them to the system prompt under "PRICES YOU GAVE EARLIER". In this script the price reply is five turns back by the time "what did you say that price was" arrives, so without this the model would not have it in its history. |

No changes to retrieval, ingestion, `bot/web.py` or `data/`.

## Things the next person should know
