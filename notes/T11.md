## (a) What changed

| File | Change |
|---|---|
| `bot/guards.py` (new) | Two checks on the model's reply, done in code. (1) `drop_unlisted_prices`: removes each sentence that states a dollar amount appearing on none of the 7 crawled pages and not in the user's own messages (so "$31,000" from "like 31k" stays, but a made-up "$31,412 worst case" goes). If every sentence goes, a short "I can't confirm that figure" line replaces the reply. (2) `ensure_quote_pointer`: a reply saying the pages don't list/say/mention something, with no pointer to a quote or contact, gets "You can request a free quote from PlacidWay to ask about this." appended. `check_reply` applies both and keeps the model's `USED:` line last. |
| `bot/chat.py` | One import, and `answer()` passes the model's text through `check_reply(...)` before `_cite(...)`. One prompt rule generalised: "If the user suggests a price, total or claim that the passages do not state, never agree or start with "yes": say you can't confirm it, then give the listed figures." |

No changes to retrieval, pages, ingestion, `bot/web.py`, `bot/llm.py` or `data/`.

## (d) Risks and checks

- `trick_total`: depends on model wording; see table. If it fails, look at whether the reply opened with agreement.
- `drop_unlisted_prices` uses every number on the pages as "listed" (lenient on purpose). It does not catch an invented amount that happens to equal some number on a page, and it checks only amounts with a `$`, `USD` or "dollars" marker.
- `ensure_quote_pointer` matches English phrasing only; replies in other languages rely on the prompt.
- If guards remove a sentence, the reply can read slightly abruptly; that is preferred to stating an unlisted price.
- Token headroom at turn 7 is the smallest seen so far (see above). Other branches adding prompt text will reduce it further.

Checks I believe are wrong: none.
